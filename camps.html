Community Screening Camps

Taking diabetes awareness and screening closer to the community through organized healthcare initiatives.

Why Community Camps?

Many people may not regularly attend health check-ups. Community camps can help improve awareness and encourage people to seek appropriate healthcare.

Easy Access

Organize screening activities in accessible community locations.

Early Awareness

Help participants understand diabetes risk factors and the importance of professional testing.

Referral Support

Guide participants with concerns towards qualified healthcare professionals.

Who Can Organize These Camps?

DiaCare Connect can be presented as a student-designed concept for collaboration with healthcare organizations.

🏥

Hospitals

Support community awareness and supervised screening activities.

💊

Pharmacies

Provide an accessible location for health education and referral guidance.

🤝

NGOs

Help coordinate community participation and awareness campaigns.

🎓

Medical Colleges

Contribute trained supervision and healthcare education.

How a Camp Can Be Organized

A simple model for planning a community screening event.

01

Planning

Select a suitable venue, arrange trained supervision and prepare the screening materials.

02

Community Awareness

Inform residents about the camp through posters, announcements and community organizations.

03

Participant Registration

Explain the activity, obtain consent and register participants confidentially.

04

Screening and Education

Conduct the approved screening workflow under appropriate healthcare supervision.

05

Referral and Follow-up

Provide referral information and encourage appropriate professional assessment where needed.

Healthcare Supervision Matters

This is a proposed educational camp model, not an independently operated medical service. Any real screening event must be planned with qualified healthcare professionals, appropriate consent, privacy safeguards, hygiene procedures and reliable referral arrangements. Urine glucose strips cannot diagnose or rule out diabetes.
