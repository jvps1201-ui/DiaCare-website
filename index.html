THE PROBLEM

Why Does Early Screening Matter?

Diabetes can develop without noticeable symptoms. Many people may remain unaware of their condition until they experience health complications.

♥

Limited Awareness

Many people do not understand diabetes, its risk factors or the importance of screening.

⌕

Access to Screening

Some communities face difficulties accessing convenient and affordable health screening services.

✚

Delayed Detection

Lack of awareness and screening opportunities can delay medical evaluation.

OUR PROPOSED SOLUTION

A Simple Approach to Community Screening

Our Community Diabetes Screening Kit combines a risk questionnaire, waist measurement, preliminary urine glucose testing and referral guidance.

It is designed to help community health camps and pharmacy partners identify people who may need further medical evaluation.
