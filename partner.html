Our Pharmacy Partnership Proposal

A simple outline of what each partner could contribute.

What We Propose

  • A structured screening workflow
  • Educational risk questionnaire
  • Recording and referral forms
  • Community awareness material
  • Estimated 15-minute workflow per participant

What We Request

  • A suitable space for the activity
  • Qualified healthcare supervision
  • Appropriate hygiene arrangements
  • Professional referral support
  • Help informing the local community

Our Shared Goal

To make diabetes awareness more accessible and encourage people to seek timely, appropriate healthcare.

Partnership and Safety

DiaCare Connect is a student project concept. Any real-world implementation would require suitable healthcare partners, qualified supervision, appropriate approvals, privacy safeguards and safe screening procedures. The prototype does not independently diagnose or treat diabetes.
