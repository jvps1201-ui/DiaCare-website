How Will We Generate Revenue?

Our proposed model uses a small service fee to support screening operations.

Pharmacy Partnership

A pharmacy provides an appropriate space and helps coordinate the service with trained healthcare personnel.

Screening Service

Participants may pay a proposed ₹100 service fee, subject to partner approval and local requirements.

Community Sponsorship

NGOs, CSR programmes and community organizations may help sponsor free or subsidized screening events.

Break-Even Calculation

How many screenings are needed to recover the initial investment?

Initial investment ₹30,000
Fee per screening ₹100
Variable cost per screening ₹40
Contribution per screening ₹60

Break-Even Formula

Break-even screenings = Initial investment ÷ Contribution per screening

₹30,000 ÷ ₹60 = 500

Financial Assumptions

All financial values are preliminary project estimates, not verified market quotations. Actual costs, service fees and break-even timelines will depend on supplier prices, pharmacy agreements, staffing, local regulations and participant demand. Screening and referral decisions must remain independent of financial incentives.
