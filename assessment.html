Diabetes Risk Awareness

A simple educational questionnaire to demonstrate how community screening can identify people who may benefit from professional health assessment.

Important information

This is a student-made demonstration. Its questions and scoring system are not clinically validated. It cannot diagnose diabetes or determine whether someone has the condition.

Demo only: Please do not enter real personal or medical information.

1. What is the person's age group?

2. Is there a family history of diabetes?

3. Has the person been told they have high blood pressure?

4. Has the person previously been told they have high blood sugar?

5. How would you describe the person's physical activity?

6. What is the waist measurement assessment?

The relevant threshold depends on the person's characteristics and local clinical guidance.
