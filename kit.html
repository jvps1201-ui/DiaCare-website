ABOUT THE PROTOTYPE

What Is Our Screening Kit?

A Simple Community-Based Approach

Our Community Diabetes Screening Kit is a proposed collection of basic screening materials intended to support early identification of people who may need further diabetes evaluation.

It combines a risk questionnaire, waist measurement, preliminary urine glucose testing, recording sheets and referral cards.

The kit is designed for organized use by trained personnel in appropriate healthcare settings.

✚

Our Main Objective

Make preliminary diabetes risk screening more accessible, organized and affordable at the community level.

SCIENTIFIC RESPONSIBILITY

Understanding the Limitations

Important Medical Considerations

  • Urine glucose strips cannot reliably detect all people with diabetes.
  • A negative urine result does not rule out diabetes.
  • The questionnaire is a proposed risk-awareness tool, not a validated diagnostic instrument.
  • Appropriate blood glucose testing or HbA1c is needed for medical evaluation.
  • Screening and referrals should be conducted with suitable healthcare supervision.
